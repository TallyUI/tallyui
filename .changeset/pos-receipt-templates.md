New receipt templates: `renderReceiptHtml` turns a `ReceiptData` into sanitised HTML with a default 80 mm or 58 mm template, and `receiptTemplateData`, `renderThermalPreview` and `sanitizeHtml` let an app render its own thermal-XML Mustache templates. QR codes and barcodes render as placeholders for now.
