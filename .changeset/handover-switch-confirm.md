Adds what an app needs to confirm a cashier switch, a register switch or a sign-out: `switchWarnings()` lists what the switch leaves behind (unsent sales and till updates, parked sales, a partly paid sale, an open register), and `handoverSlip()` builds the hand-over slip with a printable X-report envelope. In `@tallyui/components`, `SwitchConfirm` asks with WCPOS v2's wording and names each warning, and `HandoverSlip` shows the slip.
