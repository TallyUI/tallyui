The till's second-device display service (ADR-085 U4, #684), exported from the package root. `createTillDisplayService` takes a connector's `DisplaySignalling`, the cashier session, the till's device id, the store's `DisplayAdvertisement` and a `configFor` per display. It lists the displays this till owns every `LIST_POLL_MS` and on `refresh()`, keeps one WebRTC link and one display link per display, and fans each `showCart` and `showPayment` out to all of them (d9), replaying the current sale into a link made later. A link that drops, or stays connecting past `CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS`, is replaced at the next successful list. While a display's channel is not open, `createSnapshotSender` posts its snapshot fallback (d8): at most one POST a second on the trailing edge, the same body again every 20 s, no re-send of a refused body, the 200 KiB cap with the note cut first, no POST for a display the registry reports offline, and an idle snapshot that clears a recent cart from the server. When the server is lost the service goes `offline` (d11): open links carry on, and no link is made and no snapshot posted until a list succeeds. `forbidden`, or no cashier session, pauses every server call; `not_found` drops that display. The service is a `DisplayLink`, so `createSaleDisplayPublisher` drives it.
