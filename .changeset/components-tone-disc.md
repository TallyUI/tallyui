`ToneDisc`: the round tone mark a state leads with, as in WCPOS v2: a wash and one glyph per tone (`ok`, `warning`, `bad`, `info`, `muted`), sizes `sm` 44, `default` 56 and `lg` 96, and an `icon` override. Adds the `CheckIcon`, `AlertTriangleIcon`, `AlertCircleIcon` and `InfoCircleIcon` glyphs. Needs `@tallyui/theme` with the tone tokens.
