A camera barcode scanner (#720, v2's scan button and inline camera panel). `Catalogue` takes `cameraScan` (default false) and `cameraScanner`; with both it puts a `CameraScanButton` beside the search box that opens a `CameraScannerPanel`. The panel calls no pos hook: the app owns `useCameraScan` (inside its `ScanHubProvider`) and passes `{ onDetected: camera.offer, onOpen: camera.reset, scanCount: camera.scanCount }`, so accepted decodes go to the scan hub as `{ source: { kind: 'camera' } }` and the app's `useBarcodeScanner` receives them like device scans. The panel shows v2's status copy, a permission card for native apps, a flash on each `scanCount` change, and a resize handle with increment and decrement actions and a web pointer drag, reporting the height through `onHeightChange`. On the web, `ScannerViewfinder` streams `getUserMedia` into a `<video>` and decodes with the `BarcodeDetector` global when present, or with an injected `decoder` of the same `detect` shape; native apps pass their own `viewfinder`. `@tallyui/pos` adds `useCameraScan` (returning `CameraScan`: `offer`, `reset`, `scanCount`), the pure `startDecodeLoop`, `nativeBarcodeDetector`, `cameraErrorStatus`, `detectorFormatToSymbology` and their constants, and `createScanSession` takes `continuous`, under which a refused repeat restarts its cooldown, so an item resting in view is scanned once.
