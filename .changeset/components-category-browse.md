`Catalogue` takes `categoryBrowse: 'chips' | 'tiles'` (default `'chips'`). With `'tiles'` and `showCategoryNav`, the catalogue opens on WCPOS v2's browse root: All products, then each category as a `CategoryTile` poster with its product count and picture. A tile opens its products under a "Categories › name" breadcrumb whose first crumb goes back.
