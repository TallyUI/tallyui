New `CashierSheet`: WCPOS v2's cashier sheet on the right edge, with the header (avatar, name and the store · register · "since" line), "Your sales today" and Sign out. It displays what the app passes and calls the app back; Sign out never closes the sheet itself, so the app can ask through `SwitchConfirm` first.
