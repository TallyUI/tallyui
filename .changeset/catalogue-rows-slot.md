`Catalogue` takes `rowsSlot` (a `ReactNode`, default none): WCPOS v2's rows between the filter chips and the products (its `ListColumn` `rows`), for notices such as "Price check only until the register is open.". It renders under the header, above the grid, the table or the category tiles, with a 16 px gutter around and between the rows. Without it the catalogue renders as before.
