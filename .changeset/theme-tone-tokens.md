Tone tokens from WCPOS v2, light and dark: `ok`, `ok-bg`, `warning-text`, `warning-bg`, `bad`, `bad-bg` (classes such as `bg-ok-bg` and `text-bad`). `ok` equals `success` and `bad` equals `destructive`; the `-bg` tokens are the washes behind a tone's glyph or band. No existing token changes.
