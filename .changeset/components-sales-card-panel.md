Adds `SalesCardPanel`: the Sales room's Payments, Top products, Categories and Taxes cards open their figure in full in a right-hand panel, with a title, an optional scope line, a table with a total row, the order count and gross (and any missing cost) in the footer, Close, and Export CSV when `onExportCsv` is given. A `SalesCards` head that opens a panel is now 44 pt tall, dims on press and shows a chevron after its figure; without `onOpenCard` the cards are unchanged.
