Adds the app shell from WCPOS v2: `AppShell` puts a `NavRail` beside the page on windows at least 768 wide and 480 tall, and a `NavDrawer` on phones, with `aria-current` on the active item and a focus-trapped drawer that closes on Escape or a backdrop press. `RegisterBar` gains optional `storeName`, `cashier`, `syncStatus` and `onOpenMenu` props, and shows the menu button on its own inside a phone shell.
