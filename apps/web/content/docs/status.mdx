Three point-of-sale apps are built on Tally UI: [tallyui-woocommerce](https://github.com/wcpos/tallyui-woocommerce) for WooCommerce, [MedusaPOS](https://medusapos.com) for Medusa and [VendurePOS](https://vendurepos.com) for Vendure. Each has a live demo: [WooCommerce](https://tallyui-woocommerce.vercel.app), [Medusa](https://demo.medusapos.com/demo) and [Vendure](https://demo.vendurepos.com/demo).

Each **works** links the merged pull request, or the test, that shows the feature. **Not yet** means the app does not do it today. "Works" means merged into the app's main branch and running in its live demo; it may not be in a tagged release yet. Checked on 2026-10-06.
