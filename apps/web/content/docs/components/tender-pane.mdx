---
title: TenderPane
description: TenderPane is the tender pane for a host that owns the tender reducer, with the balance headline, payment legs, method tiles, keypad, quick-cash chips, Take and Complete sale.
---

## Import

```tsx
import { TenderPane } from '@tallyui/components';
```

## Usage

```tsx
import { useReducer } from 'react';
import { initTenderState, tenderReducer, useSale } from '@tallyui/pos';
import { TenderPane } from '@tallyui/components';

// Inside a component under TaxProvider, while sale.stage.kind is 'tender'.
const sale = useSale(settings, { registerId, cashierRef });
const [state, dispatch] = useReducer(tenderReducer, { methodId: 'cash', balanceMinor: sale.order.balanceDueMinor }, initTenderState);
<TenderPane sale={sale} state={state} dispatch={dispatch} />
```

`TenderPane` is the pane inside [Tender](/docs/components/tender), laid out as WCPOS v2's. Use `Tender` unless the host keeps the tender reducer itself. It renders nothing outside the tender stage.

The headline's caption reads To pay, or Remaining once money is paid (pending legs never count). The balance sits beside it when it differs from the entry, and the entry is the large figure underneath. Under the caption are the amount paid so far and any change. Each payment leg shows its method, amount and status, and a leg the till took shows the time it was taken; Paid in full names the time of the last payment. The next pending leg is marked Next, and Cancel payment shows where the method can void the leg.

Pass `ledger={false}` when the host shows the legs in the cart column with Cart's `tenderLedger`, as WCPOS v2 does.

While a balance remains, the cashier picks Cash or Card terminal and sets the entry on the keypad, where digits shift in from the right, or, for cash, with a quick-cash chip: the exact amount, then the next 5, 10 and 50. A method that gives no change, such as Card terminal, gets one Full balance chip instead, which sets the entry to the amount due (Exact while a split plan is active). A chip or a keypad press only sets the entry. A hint under it reads the change, what is left after a part payment, or that the method can't give change. `Take {amount} in {method}` records the leg with `sale.addTender`, and the entry is pre-typed with the next leg's amount. Card terminal also takes a terminal reference.

While something is due, Split on the headline shows Split the rest with 2 to 6 equal ways; after a typed amount, the ways divide what is left after this payment. A way sets the plan and the entry is pre-typed with each share. A method tile keeps the share that is due, and pressing Split again clears the plan.

With `percentSplit`, the split strip starts with Percent. Tap it to choose 10, 20, 25 or 50 %, or use the keypad to type a whole percent. The percent is of what is still due: a payment in motion is not counted again. The pane rounds the share half-up to the cent: 50 % of €10.01 takes €5.01. A share over 100 % takes the rest; a share of 0 takes nothing and says so. Cash rounding applies to the share as it does to a typed amount. After each take, or when you press Split again, the entry is money again. If `percentSplit` turns off while a percent is entered, the entry is money again.

Complete sale is enabled once nothing is due and no leg is in motion, and calls `sale.complete`. Back calls `sale.cancelTender`. When money was taken here, it first opens the cancel-payment view, WCPOS v2's. The view lists each payment: Return cash to the customer (the amount the payment kept, after its change), Void on its method, or Refund from the order for a payment the till cannot void. Keep taking payment closes it. Cancel and void voids the payments it can and returns to the cart; a payment that can only be refunded stays on the sale, which completes at a later checkout or is parked. With nothing to void, the view offers Back instead. The sale's error and its Continue control show as the sale state requires.

A held leg, one taken before the sale was reopened or one the store or a closed register's closure already holds, shows no Cancel payment and no terminal reference input. On a reopened sale the store already covers, each payment taken here shows Cancel payment when its method can void it, or a Refund line when it cannot, and then the sale can only be parked.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `sale` | `ReturnType<typeof useSale>` | required | The sale from [useSale](/docs/pos) |
| `state` | `TenderState` | required | The tender reducer's state, from `tenderReducer` in `@tallyui/pos` |
| `dispatch` | `(action: TenderAction) => void` | required | The reducer's dispatch; the pane sends method picks, keypad keys, entries and recorded legs |
| `methods` | `PaymentMethodDescriptor[]` | `defaultPaymentMethods()` | The methods offered: each manual-capture descriptor gets a tile and records its wire method (`cash` or `external`) plus its own id as `methodId`; other capture modes are not shown |
| `ledger` | `boolean` | `true` | Draws the payments ledger in the pane; pass `false` when Cart's `tenderLedger` shows it in the cart column |
| `back` | `boolean` | `true` | Draws the Back button (`sale.cancelTender`); pass `false` when the host leaves the tender stage itself, as WCPOS v2's phone checkout does; when money was taken here, Back opens the cancel-payment view first |
| `confirmVoid` | `boolean` | `false` | WCPOS v2's "Cancel this payment?" before a leg is voided: Cancel payment opens a question with the leg's amount ("Return €10.00 cash to the customer" for cash, else "Void €15.00 on Card terminal"), and the leg is voided only on confirm. Default off: Cancel payment voids at once |
| `beforeVoid` | `(leg: Payment) => boolean \| void \| Promise<boolean \| void>` | --- | A host hook that runs before Cancel payment voids a leg (after Confirm with `confirmVoid`), such as writing the void to the host's money journal first; the void waits for it, `false` or a throw keeps the leg, and the leg's Cancel payment is disabled while it runs. Absent, the void is immediate. It also runs, in order, before each leg the cancel-payment view voids; `false` or a throw stops there and keeps that leg and the ones after it. |
| `afterVoid` | `(leg: Payment, voided: boolean) => void` | --- | Runs after a void that `beforeVoid` approved, with whether the leg was voided. `false` means the leg was voided, locked or finished elsewhere while the hook awaited, so the host reverses what it journalled. Not called without `beforeVoid`. |
| `beforeTake` | `(take: { methodId: string; amountMinor: number }) => boolean \| Promise<boolean>` | --- | Checks the selected method and the amount shown on Take before recording a leg. |
| `onTaken` | `(paymentId: string) => void` | --- | Reports the new leg's payment ID. |
| `offline` | `boolean` | `false` | Marks each method tile “Offline” without disabling it. |
| `percentSplit` | `boolean` | `false` | Adds Percent to the split strip. |

Only a strict `true` from `beforeTake` takes payment. Any other answer, a throw or a rejection takes nothing. The host must always settle `beforeTake`, with its own timeout; the pane never times out on its own, and a `true` that arrives after the sale changed takes nothing. If `beforeTake` never settles, Take, Back, the method tiles, Complete sale and Cancel payment stay disabled on that order until the app reloads, even after tender is cancelled and restarted or the pane remounts. A zero-due cash settle writes off the remainder without calling either hook. `onTaken` fires only for new legs this pane records.

`beforeTake` must not move money itself: a `true` that is followed by no `onTaken` was not taken. While `beforeTake` is pending, the host must not move the sale inside a transition (`startTransition`, a form action): the pane cannot see such a move before it takes ([#1468](https://github.com/TallyUI/tallyui/issues/1468)).
