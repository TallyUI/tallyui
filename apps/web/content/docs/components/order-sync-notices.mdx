---
title: OrderSyncNotices
description: OrderSyncNotices shows one sale's sync notices, the same ones OrdersList shows under each order, for an app's own order screen.
---

## Import

```tsx
import { OrderSyncNotices } from '@tallyui/components';
```

## Usage

```tsx
<OrderSyncNotices order={order} stuck={outbox.state.stuck}>
  {retryButton}
</OrderSyncNotices>
```

These are the notices [OrdersList](/docs/components/orders-list) renders under each order, in the same order:

- how long a stuck sale has gone without reaching the online store;
- the refusal sentence, with no error code and never the store's own message;
- for a WooCommerce POS 2.0 sale the store refused after it had created the order, the store order's number and how much of the sale was recorded on it at that moment (`Order #<number> was left pending on the online store. Nothing was paid there.`, or `left part paid` with the amount and the total). The notice says what the till knew at the refusal; it does not read the order's present state on the store;
- once the till has tried to cancel that store order, where the cancel stands: `Order #<number> was cancelled on the store.` or `was removed on the store.` in place of the line above, with no refusal sentence; `changed on the store: <amount> is paid there. Nothing was cancelled from this till. Check it in the store's admin.` in place of it; or, after it, `The store did not cancel order #<number>.` with the same check, or `Cancelling order #<number> on the store is not finished.` The store's own code and message never show;
- once the till has tried to record the rest of a part-paid store order, where that stands: `Order #<number> is paid on the store.` or `Order #<number> is not on the store any more; <amount> was recorded on it.` in place of the left line; `Order #<number> is already paid on the store.` or `Order #<number> changed on the store: <amount> is paid there.` in place of it, followed by `<amount> was recorded from this till.` (or `Nothing was recorded from this till.`) and, when not paid, `Check it in the store's admin.`; or, after it, `The store did not record the rest of order #<number>: <amount> of <total> is recorded there. Check it in the store's admin.`, or `Recording the rest of order #<number> on the store is not finished.` A cancelled order whose sale took money on this till lists each payment and where to give it back, then `Give back any of this you have not already given back.`;
- a sale taken after its register closed;
- local warnings;
- a customer note that was not sent;
- the customer's tax IDs, when the sale went to the store below order.create 12;
- the order's custom fields (meta), on the same terms;
- the lines whose edited SKU was not sent, by name (`The edited SKU of <names> couldn't be sent to the store.`);
- your `children` (OrdersList puts its Retry button here);
- the store's warnings.

It returns a fragment, so the notices stack in whatever container you give them.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `order` | `PosOrder` | required | The sale whose notices to show |
| `stuck` | `OutboxState['stuck']` | — | The outbox's `state.stuck`; without it, no "hasn't reached the online store" line is shown |
| `children` | `ReactNode` | — | Rendered between the notes and the store's warnings, where OrdersList puts Retry |
