### How much of this is the fixes

Read the delta with care. The three criteria nobody fixed rose too (+0.50, +0.67, +0.83), so
about +0.7 of the lift is panel variance between two blind runs, not code.
- **Docs** is the one clear gain. It rose +1.33, more than any untouched criterion. No judge names
  `pos.mdx` any more, and judge e cites it as the typechecked page. The worst docs defect moved to
  `connectors.mdx`.
- **Stability and consistency** rose by about the noise. Their worst defects moved:
  - stability: from the poll constants to `REGISTER_SESSION_MIGRATION_CLOSE_WAIT_MS` and the RxDB
    schemas on the pos root;
  - consistency: from components' payment names to the money converters, Shopify's missing
    factory and the two currency providers.

No fix lowered the panel mean: no criterion fell, and no judge named a fixed line as a defect.
Judge d's stability note mentions `public-surface.test.ts` as a guard that is too small, which is
a scope remark, not a regression. So no PR is closed.

## What is left

- **Most agreed remaining defect:** `TallyConnector.sync` is deprecated but required, while
  `replication` is optional (b, c, d, e, f). This is proposed breaking change 3 in round 1.
- **Next most agreed:**
  - the pos root exports RxDB schemas and the register-session migration knob (a, d, e, f). They
    sit in `packages/pos/src/register/`, the W4 lane's, so this goes to that lane.
  - `connectors.mdx`'s custom-connector example does not compile (b, d, e, f). That page belongs
    to the docs lane (#860 is open on it), so this goes there, together with adding
    `connectors.mdx` to the snippet test.
- New proposed breaking changes (desk first), adding to round 1's list:
  7. **Shopify factory:** add `createShopifyConnector()` (non-breaking), then deprecate and
     remove the static `shopifyConnector`, as the other three connectors did.
  8. **One currency formatter:** pos `CurrencyProvider`/`useCurrencyFormatter`/`CurrencyFormat`
     fold into core's `MoneyFormatProvider`/`useFormatMoney`/`MoneyFormat`.
